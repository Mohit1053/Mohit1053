## What I work on

At Times Internet:

- **Personalised home feed.** LLM content enrichment, user-affinity scoring and rule-based ranking. Per-article enrichment cost down 80% and LLM card latency down 74%. Live for the first user cohorts.
- **Voice-AI calling platform.** Speech recognition, LLM dialogue and speech synthesis on GPU inference, with sub-700 ms voice-to-voice latency. Built on 36K+ real call transcripts; it tripled outbound call volume.
- **Newsroom AI-copy detection.** Two zero-shot detectors running in shadow mode, each at no more than 1% false flags.
- **Subscription lead engine.** Two calibrated gradient-boosted models, for never-subscribed and lapsed readers, that produce one ranked lead list every day.
- **Content operations.** A clipper that cuts 30-90 s clips from 7-8 hour masterclass recordings, an image-tagging API built on a CLIP, BLIP and DETR ensemble, and AI automation that cut manual ops effort by 40%.
- **Team.** I lead a cross-functional AI team (hiring, sprint planning, design and code reviews) and led the architecture of its campaign voice agents, AI video studio and audience-data pipelines.

This work lives in private company repositories.

Outside work, I am the founder and product lead of R2C, an IIIT-Delhi research-to-commercialisation platform built by 20+ student and research contributors. I architected the platform and built its social distribution engine.
